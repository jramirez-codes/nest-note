## `/pair`

> Pair a device by QR code or payload.

```text
/pair
```

Opens the camera to scan the QR the server prints. Or paste the payload
directly:

```text
/pair {"v":1,"host":"192.168.1.20","port":8443,"pin":"…","code":"…"}
```

This is the one command that works on an **unpaired** device — it's how a
device becomes paired. Full details on [the pairing page](../server/pairing.md).

## `/update-server`

> Update the paired laptop: pull the latest code, rebuild the Go server, and
> restart it.

```text
/update-server
```

From your phone, this makes the laptop:

1. `git pull` the ai-notepad checkout (`-repo-dir`, defaulting to
   `<root>/ai-notepad`),
2. rebuild the Go server,
3. restart into the new binary.

The card streams each step. The connection drops briefly during the restart and
the app reconnects on its own — you don't need to re-pair, since the
certificate and token are persisted in the server's `-dir`.

:::caution[It restarts the process you're talking to]
If the rebuild fails, the server stays on the old binary — that's the intended
safe path. But a bad commit that *builds* and then crashes on boot will leave
you with nothing to reconnect to until you fix it at the laptop.
:::
